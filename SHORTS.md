# YouTube Shorts: upload sheets

One sheet per Short, the same way as `YOUTUBE.md`. Every number comes from `python3 tools/physics.py` (via
`YOUTUBE.md`'s physics list). Files: `out/shorts/<id>.mp4` (`node shorts.mjs <id>`).

## S1 · Sunset on Europa (`out/shorts/S1.mp4`, 17.0 s, 1080×1920)

### Title
**The Sun Doesn't Set on Europa. It Falls Into Jupiter. #Shorts**

Alternatives: "Sunset on Europa Is an Eclipse #Shorts" · "What Sunset Looks Like From Europa #Shorts"

### Description
```
On Europa the Sun never touches the horizon. It sinks into Jupiter, and the day ends in an eclipse.

Jupiter hangs in the same spot in Europa's sky forever, 24 times wider than our Moon. 40 hours of sunlight pass in 10 seconds, then the Sun slides behind Jupiter, and Jupiter's atmosphere glows as a red ring around the night side.

Not AI. Computed: real physical constants, path-traced in Blender Cycles.

Full film (2 min, 4K): https://youtu.be/aJyAfv9b_7I

#Europa #Jupiter #Space #Eclipse #Physics #Shorts
```

### Tags
```
europa, jupiter, sunset on europa, sun falls into jupiter, jupiter eclipse, europa moon, space, real physics, path tracing, blender cycles, not ai, shorts
```

### Settings
- **Related video**: Europa · https://youtu.be/aJyAfv9b_7I. This puts a link under the Short to the long film.
- Category: Science & Technology · Audience: not made for kids · **Studio → "AI use": No** (as both films).
- Visibility: Public. Upload at the same time of day as the films, or when Studio's "When your viewers are on
  YouTube" chart peaks.
- Thumbnail: Studio on desktop can't set a custom one for a Short. In the YouTube app (Create → after choosing the
  file → the cover button) pick the **first second** (the full Jupiter over the ice) or the red ring.
- Captions: burned in; no subtitle track.
- Comments: on. A pinned first comment helps:
  `Every number here is computed, not guessed. Full film: https://youtu.be/aJyAfv9b_7I`

### What to read after 7 days (Studio → the Short → Analytics)
- **Viewed vs. swiped away** (Shorts' version of CTR): aim for 70 % or more viewed.
- **Average percentage viewed**: above 100 % means people let it loop.
- **Related video clicks** and the Europa film's traffic source "Shorts feed".
- Compare with the Io baseline (CTR 2.4 %, 41 % viewed) and with S2 (uploaded the same day).

## S2 · Io and Europa's shadow (`out/shorts/S2.mp4`, 10.5 s, 1080×1920)

Alternatives: "A Moon Eclipsed by a Moon, Seen From a Moon #Shorts" · "Io Crossing Jupiter, Seen From Europa #Shorts"

```
From Europa, Io crosses the face of Jupiter every 85 hours. Then it goes black: it has flown into Europa's own shadow.

81 minutes in 9.5 seconds. Io is 0.84° wide in Europa's sky, 1.6 times our Moon. Europa's shadow covers 54 % of it. On Jupiter, two black dots: Io's shadow, and ours.

We stood on Io in the last film. This is Io from the next moon out.

Not AI. Computed: real physical constants, path-traced in Blender Cycles.

Full film (2 min, 4K): https://youtu.be/aJyAfv9b_7I
```
