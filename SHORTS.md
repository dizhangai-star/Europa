## S1 · Sunset on Europa (`out/shorts/S1.mp4`, 17.0 s, 1080×1920)

### Title
**The Sun Doesn't Set on Europa. It Falls Into Jupiter. #Shorts**

Alternatives: "Sunset on Europa Is an Eclipse #Shorts" · "What Sunset Looks Like From Europa #Shorts"

### Description
```
On Europa the Sun never touches the horizon. It sinks into Jupiter, and the day ends in an eclipse.

Jupiter hangs in the same spot in Europa's sky forever, 24 times wider than our Moon. 40 hours of sunlight pass in 10 seconds, then the Sun slides behind Jupiter, and Jupiter's atmosphere glows as a red ring around the night side.

Not AI. Computed: real physical constants, path-traced in Blender Cycles.

Full film (2 min, 4K): https://youtu.be/aJyAfv9b_7I

#Europa #Jupiter #Space #Eclipse #Physics #Shorts
```
