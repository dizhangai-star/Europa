On Europa, the Sun never sets. It falls into Jupiter.

Jupiter hangs in the same spot in Europa's sky forever, 24 times wider than our Moon. Once every 3.55 days the Sun slides behind it and the day ends in an eclipse. Then a probe melts down through 20 km of ice, into an ocean no sunlight has ever reached.

▸ Not AI. Not artist's impression. Computed.
Every number on screen and behind it (sizes, angles, light levels, temperatures, pressures, how fast ice melts and refreezes, how far light travels through water) comes from published physical constants, worked out in code. Every frame is path-traced in Blender Cycles: light from a true-size Sun and from Jupiter, bounced through ice and water as real light would be. No image or video AI was used.

The physics in the film:
• Europa is tidally locked: Jupiter never moves in its sky. It is 12.3° wide; from Conamara Chaos it sits 3.5° above the horizon, partly below it, forever
• Sunlight is 1/27 of Earth's. A day lasts 85 hours
• At this site the Sun never touches the horizon: it sinks into Jupiter about 10° up. Sunset is an eclipse, 2.9 hours long, with Jupiter's atmosphere glowing as a red ring
• Io crosses Jupiter's face every 85 hours; in this alignment Europa's own shadow falls on Io and darkens half of it
• No air: liquid water boils and freezes at the same moment; a chip of ice thrown at 3 m/s rises 2.6 m (on Earth: 0.3 m); radiation is 5.4 sieverts a day
• The ice: −173 °C at the surface, −2 °C at the bottom, 242 bar of ice overhead. A 10 kW probe needs 2.9 years to melt through 20 km, and the hole freezes shut about 2 hours behind it. Below 3 km the ice is clear: the lamp's glow becomes a beam, then darkness
• The water: red light is gone within metres, blue reaches about 100 m. The ocean is about 100 km deep, twice the water of all Earth's oceans; its floor is under more pressure than the bottom of the Mariana Trench
• Time-lapse clocks are on screen: 2.9 years of drilling pass in 20 seconds

What is a choice, not a measurement:
• Nobody has measured the ice or the ocean yet. 20 km of ice and 100 km of water are the middle of the scientific estimates (ice 3 to 30+ km, ocean 60 to 150 km)
• No probe has flown. Ours follows NASA/JPL concepts (PRIME, Europa Tunnelbot)
• The astronaut is there to give scale
